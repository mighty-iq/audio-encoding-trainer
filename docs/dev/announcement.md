**Earshot: a free listening trainer for the moments conversations break**

Built by the Chimera community. Earshot trains following one voice in a loud room, catching words across echoing halls and understanding unfamiliar talkers, plus holding on to what you heard.

**Eight exercises**
- **Crowd**: follow one voice while others talk over it
- **Far**: catch words across rooms, halls and stations
- **Strange voices**: other-language model voices, fast, whispered, phone-line and robot-like speech
- **Repair**: rebuild words lost under bursts of noise
- **Echo**: hear a message once in a busy place, answer after a pause
- **Seek**: move through a noisy room to find a faint voice (a new bet)
- **Voices**: get to know a cast of voices, then find your friend in a crowd (a new bet)
- **Missions**: one real-world listening tactic a week

Every exercise holds you at your edge on several dials at once: how loud and how alike the other voices are, how far away and how echoey the room. Each one names the carry-over bet it makes. No streaks, and no score while you listen.

**The Lab** checks what carries over, with voices, rooms and formats you never train on, and shows your own trend with its margin of error. **The Weekly Night Out** is one seeded challenge that everyone plays identically: four stops, six trials each, and a card to share.

Free and open source. Runs in any browser on phone or desktop, installs to your home screen, works offline, and keeps your data on your device.
