# Asset licences (audio and word data)

The app's code is MIT and its texts are CC BY-SA 4.0 (see the repository licence files). The audio clips in
`audio/pack-*.js` and the generated data in `data/` come from the sources below. Every clip's source is
recorded in `data/manifest.js` (`voice` field) and `data/voices.js` (`src`, `licence`). All audio was rendered
or converted at build time by `tools/audio/` (see `docs/dev/STIMULI.md`); no speech synthesiser, phonemiser
or speech recogniser ships with the app.

|---|---|---|---|
| Kokoro-82M v1.0 (hexgrad), ONNX int8 export from the kokoro-onnx releases | Apache License 2.0 (model weights) | English voices (US and UK) and voices of other languages reading English, rendered as phrases and sentences | "Speech synthesised with Kokoro-82M by hexgrad, Apache License 2.0." |
| Piper voice en_US-libritts-high (Rhasspy Piper project; sherpa-onnx mirror) | Voice trained from scratch on LibriTTS train-clean-360, CC BY 4.0 | A spread of speakers chosen by pitch and clarity | "Voices synthesised with the Piper voice en_US-libritts-high, trained on LibriTTS (Zen et al., 2019; CC BY 4.0), which derives from LibriSpeech and LibriVox recordings." |

synthetic audio produced with closed commercial speech synthesisers. The weights are Apache-2.0; we state this
so players know where the synthetic voices come from.

All word lists, sentences, messages, stories and questions written for this app are original (CC BY-SA 4.0
with the other texts), and none is copied from a clinical or commercial test. The exceptions are the corpora's
own texts: the twelve CREMA-D sentences (Lab only) and the LibriVox readings (background talkers). The Lab's
matrix words were chosen by us and checked against the published British English matrix test (Hall, 2006,
University of Southampton MSc dissertation, Table 2.1): they share no word and no verb lemma with it (the
American English list could not be opened for the check).
