## Core (a design decision depends on it)
1. Whitton et al. 2017, Current Biology (audiomotor training) · doi:10.1016/j.cub.2017.09.014 (PMC5997394) · game
   mechanics, adaptive rule, SNRs, test materials, effect sizes, decay · Seek uses our own closed-loop search design.
2. Brandewie & Zahorik 2010, JASA (prior room listening) · doi:10.1121/1.3436565 (R2 read it in full; R3 did not) ·
   room set, carrier length, SNRs · Far plays ≥1 s of the room before the target (R2 notes hold the numbers).
3. Wright et al. 2010 J Neurosci; Wright, Baese-Berk, Marrone & Bradlow 2015 JASA · doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.0487-10.2010;
   doi:10.1121/1.4927411 · ratio and timing of passive exposure to practice · 20–30 s listen-only interludes.
4. Brungart 2001, JASA · doi:10.1121/1.1345696 · scores vs TMR by masker type · same-sex ≈20 points, same-voice ≈40
   points harder (via Bronkhorst 2015) order Crowd's similarity steps.
5. Darwin, Brungart & Simpson 2003, JASA · doi:10.1121/1.1616924 · scores per F0 and VTL step · steps up to 12 st and
   ratio 1.34, super-additive (secondary).
6. Holmes, To & Johnsrude 2021 (R3 read it; R2 did not), Holmes et al. 2018, Johnsrude et al. 2013, Nygaard et al. 1994 ·
   minutes of voice training and benefit in competing speech · ~10–60 min gives 10–15 % (R3).
7. Cameron & Dillon 2011, JAAA (spatial training) · doi:10.3766/jaaa.22.10.6 · azimuths, steps, dB changes ·
   headphone spatial dial adaptive on target level.
8. Smits, Goverts & Festen 2013, JASA (digits in noise) · retest SD and learning (secondary sources disagree) ·
   SD 0.7–1 dB; one practice run before baseline.
